---
title: "A Note on Titles, Masters & Everything Else"
slug: "titles-note"
description: "On the quiet path Tantrasya chooses instead of grand titles, endless modalities and louder claims of spiritual mastery."
---

Somewhere along the way, spiritual practice acquired a rather impressive collection of titles.

First came the healer.

Then the master healer.

Then the spiritual master.

Then the grandmaster.

Then the master of multiple modalities.

And eventually, the person who teaches the masters how to master.

At this rate, one day we may need another qualification just to explain the qualification we already have.

There is nothing wrong with learning, studying different traditions, or developing a sincere spiritual practice. The problem begins when titles, certificates, modalities and grand claims become more important than the practice itself.

Tantrasya has no interest in that race.

There is no collection of impressive titles here that needs to be placed before or after a name to make the work sound important. There is no need to present a long list of modalities as proof of spiritual superiority.

**The work does not need to be performed for an audience.**

A person who approaches paranormal or occult practice may already be dealing with something deeply personal. They do not necessarily need another grand title, another dramatic promise, or another person claiming to have all the answers.

Sometimes, they simply need someone willing to listen carefully, take their experience seriously, investigate what can be investigated, and approach the matter with discretion and respect.

That is the kind of practice Tantrasya chooses to follow.

Less branding.  
Less performance.  
Less claiming.  

More attention.  
More practice.  
More respect for the work.  

And perhaps, just occasionally, it is perfectly all right to let the work speak for itself.
