The Practice

Tantrasya is an independent practice focused on paranormal investigation and traditional occult practice.

My interest in the paranormal and occult has developed over years of personal study, observation and experience. The work has gradually taken a more focused direction — toward understanding unexplained experiences, investigating what can be examined, and working with traditional occult practices where appropriate.

There is no need here for grand titles or claims of mastery. The practice is simply about taking the subject seriously, treating people with respect, and approaching each situation with discretion.

Privacy & Discretion

People sometimes approach this work during difficult or frightening circumstances. Their experiences are theirs, not marketing material.

Tantrasya therefore maintains discretion around individual cases and does not publish private client experiences simply to demonstrate credibility.
