Paranormal Investigation

Some experiences are difficult to explain. Strange sounds, recurring disturbances, unexplained movements, unusual perceptions, or a persistent feeling that something is not quite right can leave people wondering what is actually happening.

Tantrasya approaches such situations as investigations rather than assumptions.

What May Be Investigated

Situations that warrant a closer look

  • Reported unusual activity within a home or property
  • Recurring unexplained disturbances
  • Reported apparitions or perceived presences
  • Unusual sounds, movements or environmental occurrences
  • Experiences associated with a particular location
  • Situations where a paranormal or occult explanation is being considered

The Investigation

The exact approach depends on the circumstances.

It may involve:

  • Listening carefully to the account
  • Understanding the history and circumstances surrounding the experience
  • Examining the location
  • Environmental observation
  • Appropriate investigative equipment
  • Documentation of relevant observations
  • Considering ordinary explanations alongside paranormal possibilities

Investigation before conclusion. A report of something unusual is a reason to investigate, not automatically a conclusion about what caused it.

When Occult Practice Becomes Relevant

Some cases extend beyond investigation.

Where appropriate, traditional tantra-mantra or other occult practices may be considered separately from the investigation itself.
