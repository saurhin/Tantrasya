Occult Practice

Tantrasya works within traditional occult practice, including tantra, mantra and ritual approaches to situations understood by the individual as having an occult or spiritual dimension.

Tantra

An exploration of traditional tantric practice as part of a broader occult framework, approached with attention to the nature and circumstances of the situation.

Mantra

Traditional mantra practices may form part of an occult or spiritual approach where appropriate.

Ritual Practice

Ritual work is undertaken according to the nature and circumstances of a situation rather than as a standardised answer to every problem.

Protection & Clearing

Traditional protective and clearing practices may be considered in situations where an occult or spiritual dimension is believed to be involved.

A Measured Approach

Not an answer to every unexplained event.

Occult practice is not presented as a solution to every situation. The nature of the situation matters, and the appropriate approach depends on the circumstances.
